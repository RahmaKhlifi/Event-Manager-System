What is Event Management?

Event management is the application of project management to the creation and development of large-scale events such as festivals, conferences, ceremonies, weddings, formal parties, concerts, or conventions. It involves:

  • Concept development & budgeting
  • Venue selection & logistics
  • Marketing & promotion
  • On-site coordination & risk management
